Look test: HD-2D

The same room and the same 32-pixel monsters, drawn two ways. Now is how the game looks today. HD-2D keeps the pixel monsters but adds lighting (torches, glowing attacks that light up the room), soft shadows, a blur on the near and far edges like a camera lens, glow on beams and shots, and floating light motes.
