Monster redesigns

Monsters that look too much like a specific Pokémon get original new looks before release. Each keeps its name, type and moves. The big pictures show what each concept is meant to look like (switch between anime and cartoon styles below); the small one beside "In the game" is its actual 32-pixel sprite. Pick a concept for each (or mix ideas, e.g. "A with C's horns"), and it'll be drawn in all five views and animated. The anime pictures show a tougher, shōnen / seinen take on each design; the small pixel sprites are still the earlier, cuter drafts and will be redrawn in whichever spirit you pick.
