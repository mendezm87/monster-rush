Proposal: our own types

Twelve original types grown out of the world (a fallen high-tech civilization, its ruins reclaimed by nature, its data corrupted or still alive), replacing the 18 copied ones. Read the chart across: a move of the row's type does 2× (green) or ½ (red) damage to a Coreature of the column's type; blank is normal. Three of our own triangles sit beside the classic fire / water / plant one: Arcane beats Glitch beats Iron beats Arcane, and Beast beats Shade beats Arcane beats Beast.
