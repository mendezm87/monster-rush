Our 14 types

Fifteen types: plain names for the elements (Fire, Water, Electric, Ice, Plant, Earth, Wind, Metal) and our own for the seven that are only ours (Glitch, Arcane, Shade, Phantom, Beast, Brawl, and Dragonkin, added later), grown out of the world (a fallen high-tech civilization, its ruins reclaimed by nature, its data corrupted or still alive), replacing the 18 copied from Pokémon. Read the chart across: a move of the row's type does 2× (green) or ½ (red) damage to a Coreature of the column's type; blank is normal. Every matchup has a reason a player could guess (water puts fire out, metal carries the shock, you can't punch a shadow); each type's card lists them.
