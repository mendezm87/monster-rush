How to play

  • Explore the floor room by room. Walk through a doorway to go to the next room. The map in the top corner shows where you've been; the red room is the boss.
  • Doors lock when wild monsters appear. Beat them all to open the doors again. Coins and berries pop out of them: walk over them to pick them up. Berries heal.
  • Move with WASD / arrow keys, or the thumb-stick on touch.
  • Attack with 4 moves: left click, right click, Shift + left click and Shift + right click (or J K L ;), or tap the move buttons. Moves auto-aim at the nearest wild monster.
  • Swap monsters with the SWAP button or Q. In a fight the action keeps going while your next monster is thrown in, and swapping has an 8 second cooldown.
  • HP carries over between rooms. If your whole party faints, the run is over. Fainted monsters get back up with a bit of HP on each new floor.
  • Recruit rooms (blue on the map) let you pick 1 of 3 monsters to join your party (up to 6). Release monsters from the Party screen in the pause menu.
  • Level up by beating wild monsters: the monster that's out gets full XP, the rest of the party half. Starters evolve at level 6.
  • The shop (yellow on the map) sells healing and relics for coins. Treasure rooms (white) hold a free relic. Relics last the whole run and change how your moves work.
  • Beat a monster as a boss to unlock it as a starter for future runs.
  • Beat the boss to open the stairs to the next floor. Clear 3 floors to win the run.
  • Endless runs never stop: the floors keep coming and keep getting tougher. See how deep you can go.
  • Esc or P pauses, M mutes. Gamepads work too.
